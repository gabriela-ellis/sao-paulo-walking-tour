About the walks

Sampa doesn't show up on a bus tour.

São Paulo rewards people who slow down — the mortadella queue at the Mercadão, the alley that gets repainted every month in Vila Madalena, the jacarandás that turn Avenida Paulista violet for three weeks every August. We built four routes around those details, each led by a guide who walks that neighborhood on their own time, not just on tour days.

Groups stay small on purpose — eight people, one guide, no headsets. Everything is on foot at a conversational pace, with stops built in for the market stalls, the viewpoints, and the inevitable coffee.

  • Group sizeMax 8 guests, 1 guide. Private walks available for larger groups.
  • LanguagesPortuguese, English, and Spanish — guide assigned when you book.
  • WeatherWalks run rain or shine. Guides carry ponchos; you should bring shoes you don't mind getting wet.
  • Pace2–3.2km, mostly flat, with seated stops roughly every 45 minutes.

How it works

Meet, walk, taste, finish.

Meet

Show up at the meeting point on the route page. Your guide holds a small SAMPA A PÉ sign — no app to download.

Walk

A conversational pace with stops for stories, photos, and the occasional queue for a sandwich.

Taste

Market and food routes include tastings in the price — everything else is bring-your-own appetite.

Finish

Routes end near a metro station, so getting to your next stop in the city is a short ride away.
